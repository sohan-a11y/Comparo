# How to Use Comparo for Price Comparison

## Quick Start Guide

### 1. Launch the App
Open Comparo from your Android device's app drawer. You'll see a browser-like interface with three tabs at the top.

### 2. Understanding the Interface

#### Top Navigation Bar
- **Back Button** (←): Go to the previous page
- **Forward Button** (→): Go to the next page in history
- **Refresh Button** (⟳): Reload the current page
- **Home Button** (⌂): Return to platform homepage

#### URL Bar
- Type any URL or search term
- Press "Go" or Enter to navigate
- Shows the current page URL

#### Platform Tabs
- **Zepto**: Quick commerce delivery
- **Swiggy Instamart**: Grocery delivery by Swiggy
- **Blinkit**: Instant grocery delivery

### 3. Comparing Prices - Step by Step

#### Method 1: Manual Search Across Platforms
1. **Select Zepto tab**
   - Search for your desired product (e.g., "milk", "bread", "tomatoes")
   - Browse and note the price

2. **Switch to Swiggy Instamart tab**
   - Search for the same product
   - Compare the price with Zepto

3. **Switch to Blinkit tab**
   - Search for the same product again
   - Compare prices from all three platforms

4. **Make your decision**
   - Choose the platform with the best price
   - Consider delivery fees and minimum order amounts

#### Method 2: Direct Product Search
1. In the URL bar, type the product name
2. Click "Go"
3. The current platform will search for that product
4. Switch tabs to repeat the search on other platforms

### 4. Tips for Best Price Comparison

#### Search Consistently
- Use the same product name across all platforms
- Be specific (e.g., "Amul milk 1L" instead of just "milk")
- Check product sizes/weights are identical

#### Consider Additional Factors
- **Delivery Fee**: Factor in platform delivery charges
- **Minimum Order**: Check if there's a minimum order requirement
- **Delivery Time**: Faster delivery might be worth a small price difference
- **Quality & Brand**: Ensure you're comparing the same brand/quality
- **Offers & Discounts**: Look for platform-specific deals

#### Save Time
- Keep commonly purchased items' pages bookmarked in your device browser
- Note typical price ranges to quickly identify good deals
- Check multiple platforms during big sale days

### 5. Advanced Features

#### Navigation History
- Use Back/Forward buttons to revisit recently viewed pages
- Each platform maintains its own browsing history
- Helps when comparing multiple products

#### URL Bar Usage
- Direct URLs: `https://www.zepto.com/product/xyz`
- Search terms: Just type the product name
- Category browsing: Navigate to specific categories on each platform

### 6. Common Shopping Scenarios

#### Weekly Grocery Shopping
1. Make a list of items needed
2. Search for each item on all three platforms
3. Create a comparison chart (mentally or on paper)
4. Choose the platform with best overall savings
5. Consider splitting order if significant savings exist

#### Emergency Purchase
1. Search for the item on all platforms
2. Check delivery times
3. Choose fastest delivery with reasonable price
4. Order immediately from selected platform

#### Bulk Buying
1. Check if platforms offer bulk discounts
2. Compare per-unit prices
3. Factor in storage capacity
4. Choose platform with best bulk rates

### 7. Platform-Specific Tips

#### Zepto
- Often fastest delivery (10-15 minutes in some areas)
- Good for quick needs
- Check their express delivery zones

#### Swiggy Instamart
- Wide product range
- Good integration with Swiggy One membership
- Check for combo offers

#### Blinkit
- Competitive pricing on staples
- Good for household essentials
- Check for daily deals

### 8. Maximizing Savings

1. **Compare Before Every Order**
   - Prices change frequently
   - Weekend vs weekday pricing may differ
   - Time-sensitive deals

2. **Track Price Patterns**
   - Notice when platforms run sales
   - Understand seasonal pricing
   - Identify best days for specific categories

3. **Use Platform Offers**
   - Credit/debit card offers
   - First-time user discounts
   - Referral benefits

4. **Bundle Wisely**
   - Reach minimum order on cheapest platform
   - Combine similar delivery time items
   - Reduce per-item delivery cost impact

### 9. Things to Remember

- Internet connection is required for all operations
- Prices shown are real-time from platform websites
- The app doesn't store personal data or payment info
- Login to individual platforms if needed for member prices
- Some features may require platform-specific accounts

### 10. Troubleshooting

#### Page Not Loading
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Use Home button to reset to platform homepage

#### Wrong Platform Content
- Verify you're on the correct tab
- Check the URL bar shows the right platform domain
- Use Home button to return to starting page

#### Slow Performance
- Close and reopen the app
- Clear device cache (Android Settings → Apps → Comparo → Clear Cache)
- Ensure stable internet connection

## Example Comparison Workflow

Let's say you want to buy **"Amul Taaza Toned Milk 1L"**:

1. Open Comparo
2. **Zepto Tab**:
   - Search "Amul Taaza milk 1L"
   - Find it costs ₹56
   - Note delivery fee: ₹25

3. **Swiggy Instamart Tab**:
   - Search "Amul Taaza milk 1L"  
   - Find it costs ₹54
   - Note delivery fee: ₹20

4. **Blinkit Tab**:
   - Search "Amul Taaza milk 1L"
   - Find it costs ₹58
   - Note delivery fee: ₹25

5. **Comparison**:
   - Zepto: ₹56 + ₹25 = ₹81 total
   - Swiggy: ₹54 + ₹20 = ₹74 total ✓ (Best)
   - Blinkit: ₹58 + ₹25 = ₹83 total

6. **Decision**: Order from Swiggy Instamart for best price!
